I study how internal states shape behavior, and how neural circuits implement the
computations that support it. My work sits between systems neuroscience and NeuroAI:
recurrent-network agents on one side, population-level analysis of calcium imaging
and behavior on the other.

## Research Interests

**Internal-state dynamics in social behavior.**
How needs such as social homeostatic drive are represented, updated, and converted
into action. I study longitudinal isolation–reunion paradigms and the medial preoptic
nucleus (MPN) populations that track social need, using pose estimation, semantic
segmentation, CEBRA, and recurrent switching linear dynamical systems.

**Recurrent network models of adaptive behavior.**
Training RNN agents on social interaction tasks to ask what internal-state
representations and dynamics are sufficient to reproduce observed behavior and the
neural signatures that accompany it — and where they diverge.

**Predictive coding and active inference.**
Keeping prediction, latent-state inference, prediction error, and action as separable
stages before committing to an architecture. Prediction and action-dependent
prediction are the near-term targets.

**Biological plausibility of learning rules.**
Turning "could a biological circuit implement this?" into a measurable quantity, by
separating checkable facts about a rule from the judgments about which facts are
biologically good.
